> **The clearest case in this workspace of an event where the documents exist and are withheld.**

**Established.** Born Rabat 1920; among the youngest signatories of the **Independence Manifesto, 11 January 1944**; tutor to the future Hassan II; left Istiqlal on 25 January 1959 and helped form the **UNFP**; secretary and chair of the preparatory commission for the **Tricontinental Conference** (Havana, January 1966); **abducted in Paris on 29 October 1965** in an operation involving French police officers; never seen again.

**Judicial finding.** A 1967 French trial convicted two officers of kidnapping. The court identified Moroccan Interior Minister **Mohamed Oufkir** as principally responsible. The witness **Georges Figon**, who testified that Oufkir stabbed Ben Barka, was found dead — officially suicide.

**Alleged and unproven.** **Ahmed Dlimi** requesting the operation on the king's behalf. Mossad locating him and supplying logistics (Ronen Bergman, 2018) — `JOURNALISTIC`, based on Israeli sources. Czechoslovak StB contact 1961–1965 (Jan Koura, 2020) — `ACADEMIC`, from StB files, and it complicates the martyrology without touching the responsibility question.

**Unknown.** The location of the body; the manner and exact date of death; whether Hassan II gave a direct order; the full extent of French and American foreknowledge.

**Why he belongs at the centre of Phase IX.** Ben Barka is the point where the independence movement's left wing was physically removed. Everything about the Years of Lead follows from the fact that it could be done in Paris, by a French police operation, and remain officially unresolved sixty years later.
